The FAQ (Five W's Diet 🥗)

WHAT

What is AuroraAI?

A website creator, inspired by websim, that helps you generate or build websites using AI.

What does “BYOK” mean?

“Bring Your Own Key” — you provide your OpenRouter API key. You pay for what you use, no hidden fees.

Is it free?

Gratis: Maybe. If you use free models on OpenRouter, yes.
Libre: Yes. AGPL-3.0 licensed. Open source or bust.

What is Atarashi (新しい)?

It means "New." It's our upgraded client. The old auroraai-client is dead to us now.
